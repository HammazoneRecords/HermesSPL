# Three-Tier Difficulty System

The three-tier system structures philosophical content so readers can engage at their level.

## Valley (v0.3)

**Audience:** The curious newcomer who will bounce if hit with terminology.

**Rules:**
- Zero jargon. No formal terms from the framework.
- Pure narrative, metaphor, story, imagery.
- Lead with the experience the reader already has.
- Let the truth land in the body before the mind gets vocabulary for it.
- If the reader feels something shift but can't name it, you succeeded.

**Example:**
- Instead of: "Solobility = the readiness measure for truth reflection"
- Write: "There's a moment when someone tells you something true about yourself, and it lands clean. Like a stone dropping into still water. You don't flinch. You don't argue. You just... recognize it."

## Ridge (v0.6)

**Audience:** Readers who felt something in the Valley and want to go deeper.

**Rules:**
- Names and frameworks emerge naturally.
- Introduce the 8 Jhanos Gates as recognizable phases of lived experience.
- Axioms show up as observations, not laws.
- Reader builds their own vocabulary for what they've already lived.
- Still narrative-driven, but with structural scaffolding.

**Example:**
- "You know that period where everything feels stuck and nothing moves? That's a BARA-dominant phase. The structure is protecting you, but it's also suffocating you."

## Peak (v0.9)

**Audience:** Readers ready to be practitioners — to measure, diagnose, and engineer alignment.

**Rules:**
- Full formal architecture: Axioms & Invariants, traceable references, notation.
- The toolkit for Solobticians.
- Still grounded in experience, but with precision.
- Includes formal definitions, formulas, and diagnostic tools.

## The Journey

```
Feel it (v0.3) → Name it (v0.6) → Measure it (v0.9)
```

Most readers should enter at v0.3. If they're ready, they climb. If not, the Valley is enough.
