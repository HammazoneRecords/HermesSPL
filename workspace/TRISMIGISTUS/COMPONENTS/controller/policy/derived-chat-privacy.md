# Derived Chat Privacy Policy

Derived artifacts may store only: source locators, source hashes, provenance role, timestamps, paraphrases, classification, and status.

Forbidden in active derived records: source previews, raw quotations, raw message bodies, direct conversational fragments, credentials, or dashboard-visible private text.

If a generated artifact violates this rule: quarantine it, issue a privacy receipt, exclude it from all active pipelines, and re-extract from immutable source under the sanitizing contract.
